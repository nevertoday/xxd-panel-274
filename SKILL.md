## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one small symbol drawn in luminous crayon on black or deep paper, not a finished illustration. Keep a naive contour that wobbles, breaks and repeats. Candy color is a mark, a speck or a chalk fragment, not a flat fill. A few stars or rings may support the subject. Type, if any, is a tiny handmade word. Reject a complete scene, a realistic figure, a commercial cartoon logo, 3D, smooth vector and perfect geometry.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
